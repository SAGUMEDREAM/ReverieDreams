[Exploration Log]

A hot spring was discovered in a low-lying area.

Steam continuously rises from the surface,
and the temperature is noticeably higher than the surroundings.
The water itself is not murky—in fact, it appears unusually clear.

At first glance, it seemed like an ordinary natural hot spring.

That was, until I noticed the arrangement around it.

Several candles are placed sporadically along the edge of the spring.
Some have already burned out, leaving only hardened wax;
others are still slowly burning.

The flames are unsteady.
They flicker slightly under the interference of the steam,
yet never seem to go out.

No signs of human presence were found.

These candles do not appear to have been placed at random—
but rather, as if they were intentionally left behind.

I chose not to remain any longer.
