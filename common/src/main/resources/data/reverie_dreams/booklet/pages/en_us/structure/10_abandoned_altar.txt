### Align: Center

[Exploration Log: Unmarked Ruins]

I discovered it in an area with almost no signs of human activity.

At first, it was just an unusually flat stretch of ground.
Compared to the chaotic surroundings, it felt unnaturally orderly.
Following this trace further, I eventually came upon the altar.

——

[Structural Observation]

This structure was clearly man-made.

The arrangement of the stones follows a distinct pattern,
and the center appears to have been designed to hold certain items.
The surrounding layout is not random either—it resembles something used for ritual purposes.

But now—

it has all been abandoned.

Dust covers the surface, making it difficult to discern its original color.
Vines have spread along the კიდges, tightly wrapping parts of the structure.
Some areas even show signs of minor damage.

It has not been used for a very long time.

——

[Remnants]

A chest still remains beside the altar.

It is not locked.

The contents are disorganized, yet not completely looted.
Some materials have decayed or lost their function, but a portion remains recognizable—and even usable.

These items appear to have been left behind in a hurry.
Not intentionally stored, but rather—unable to be taken away in time.

——

[Preliminary Assessment]

Based on its structure and the remnants, this altar likely once served a specific function.

Now, however, it is inactive.

I attempted to place some materials back into position.
There was no response.

Perhaps certain key steps are missing,
or perhaps—it requires specific conditions.

——

[Record]

Before leaving, I surveyed the surroundings once more.

There were no additional traces.
No footprints, and no signs of recent activity.

This place has simply been left behind.

——

[Conclusion]

It is possible that this altar can still be reactivated.

But for now, I lack the necessary information to do so.

I will leave this place for the time being.

(Record ends)
