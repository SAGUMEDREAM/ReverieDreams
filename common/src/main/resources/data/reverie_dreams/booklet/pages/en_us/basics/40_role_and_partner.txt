### Align: Center
<green>Role Companion (Role)</> is a follower-type entity in this mod, functioning similarly to maid units from the Touhou Little Maid mod.

### Align: Left
Role Companions possess various abilities, including inventory management, combat, work tasks, and interaction.
In most cases, each Role Companion requires an owner. Players can tame them by feeding them cake, thereby establishing a master-servant relationship.

### Align: Center
In Survival mode, players can craft various Touhou Project character cards at the <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:gensokyo_altar'></></>, which are used to summon the corresponding Role Companions.

### Align: Left
When a Role Companion dies, it will drop a <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:role_archive'></></>.
Players can place this item in the <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:gensokyo_altar'></></> and surround it with two layers of diamonds in the recipe to revive the character.

### Align: Center
Other Controls:

### Align: Center

- Shift + Left Click (empty hand) - Stop attacking target
- Shift + Right Click - Open inventory
