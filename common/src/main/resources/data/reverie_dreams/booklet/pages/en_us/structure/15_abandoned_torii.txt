[Brief Record]

An isolated torii gate was found standing in an open area.

The structure is aged, covered in vines and weathering.
Its original color is almost entirely faded, leaving only a mottled outline.

There is no shrine nearby, nor any visible path.
There are no signs that other buildings ever stood here.

It stands alone, just like this.

As if it were something left behind—
a boundary of some kind.
