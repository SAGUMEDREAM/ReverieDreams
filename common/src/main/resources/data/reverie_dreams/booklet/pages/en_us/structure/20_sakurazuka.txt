[Exploration Log]

I paused for a while beneath a cherry blossom mound.

It is noticeably quieter here than the surrounding area.
When the wind passes through, it lifts scattered petals into the air,
only for them to quickly settle once more.
The ground shows no signs of disturbance—
it seems no one has come here for a long time.

And yet, this place does not feel “empty.”

A faint trace of spiritual energy lingers in the air.
Subtle, yet consistently present.
It does not feel recent,
but rather like something that has accumulated over time.

I attempted to locate its source.

There are no visible devices or structures,
nor any recognizable signs of ritual activity.
The energy seems dispersed throughout the surroundings—
clinging to the soil, the roots, even the fallen petals.

Perhaps this is not something that is “happening” now.

But rather, something that once existed here.

The exact cause remains unclear.
Recorded here for further investigation.
