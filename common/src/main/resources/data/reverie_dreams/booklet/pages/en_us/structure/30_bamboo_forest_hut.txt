[Exploration Log]

A small hut was discovered deep within the bamboo grove.

From a distance, a faint light could be seen from within.
In such an environment where it is easy to lose one’s sense of direction,
this kind of light stands out unnaturally.

There is a small pond beside the hut.
The water surface is calm, with several lotus leaves growing upon it.
They appear to be in good condition, as if carefully tended.
However, there are no clear signs of recent activity nearby.

The path leading here has long since faded.
Fallen leaves and new growth have covered everything,
making the original route nearly impossible to trace.

And yet, the place does not feel abandoned.

Both the plants in the pond and the persistent light within the hut
maintain a subtle sense of “life.”

I did not enter the hut.

After observing from the outside, I chose to leave.

This place seems to be sustained—
by some unknown means.
