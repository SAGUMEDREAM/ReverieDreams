### Align: Center
Touhou Mystia's Izakaya is a Touhou Project fan game, and this mod introduces it as a standalone gameplay feature.
This system adds ingredients, dishes, drinks, and cooking recipes from the original game, while also adjusting some vanilla Minecraft drops.

### Align: Left
In this mod, most ingredients are tied to specific crops; some seeds can be obtained from chests, while seafood must be acquired through fishing or gathering.
Each food item has different tags, and these tags determine its effects.

### Align: Center
### Header: Cooking
### Align: Left
The cooking system includes five main typeInstance of cookware: <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:cooking_pot'></>, <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:cutting_board'></>, <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:frying_pan'></>, <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:grill'></>, and <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:steamer'></>, each with upgraded variants.

### Align: Center
If the recipe input is incorrect or contains conflicting tags, it may produce <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:food/dark_cuisine'></>.

### Align: Left
Some recipes support equivalent substitutions using compatible items from other mods.
All cooking recipes can be viewed through the mod’s recipe manager or the Mystia's Izakaya wiki.

### Header: Alcohol
### Align: Center
Most drinks can be obtained by trading with the tavern keeper. Drinking them grants various effects and buffs, and the available selection may change daily.

### Align: Left
However, <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:drink/green_tea'></>, <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:drink/fruity_high_ball'></>, <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:drink/fruity_sour'></>, and <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:drink/qi'></> will always be available.

### Align: Center
Players can right-click a villager with a barrel to convert them into a tavern keeper.
