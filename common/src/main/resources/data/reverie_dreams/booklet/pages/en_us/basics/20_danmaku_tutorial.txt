### Align: Center

<yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:danmaku'></> refers to the danmaku (bullet hell) system commonly seen in STG (shooting) games, where dense projectiles form complex attack patterns.
In this mod, the concept of “danmaku” follows the same principle.

### Align: Left
To craft danmaku, players must use the <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:danmaku_crafting_table'></></>. The required materials are arranged as follows:

### Align: Center
- Any Dye - ×4
- <item 'reverie_dreams:danmaku_core'> - ×4
- <item 'reverie_dreams:power'> - ×35
- <item 'reverie_dreams:point'> - ×35
- <item 'reverie_dreams:danmaku_recipe_creator'> - ×1

### Align: Left
<yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:danmaku_recipe_creator'></> is a key tool used to define the shape of danmaku, and is essential for crafting them.
Players can right-click the template to construct danmaku patterns in a grid-based (pixel-like) manner.

### Align: Center

### Align: Left
Different types of danmaku have their own unique properties, but all can be upgraded using the <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:strength_table'></>.
The following upgrade materials and their effects are currently defined:

### Align: Center
- <item 'minecraft:iron_sword'> - Increases danmaku damage
- <item 'minecraft:slime_block'> - Increases the number of projectiles per shot
- <item 'reverie_dreams:speed_feather'> - Increases firing speed
- <item 'reverie_dreams:spell_card_template'> - Defines projectile trajectory
