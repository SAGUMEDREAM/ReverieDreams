<green>Fumo (ふもふも)</> refers to Touhou Project-themed plush toys produced by the Japanese company Gift, based on character designs from the doujin circle ANGELTYPE.
Their defining features include a seated pose, the iconic blank expression (ᗜˬᗜ), a soft plush texture, and officially licensed chibi-style designs. They are widely recognized as a signature “healing” merchandise within the Touhou community.

In this mod, most Fumo can be obtained in Survival mode through relatively expensive trades.
Once placed, a Fumo will automatically face the player's current position and can be oriented in 16 directions. Right-clicking a Fumo will also trigger its unique sound.

Fumo can only be obtained by trading with a Fumo Merchant. In general, the merchant’s available trades will change daily.

A Fumo Merchant can be created by using <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:fumo_license'></></> on a villager (right-click), converting them into the corresponding profession.
