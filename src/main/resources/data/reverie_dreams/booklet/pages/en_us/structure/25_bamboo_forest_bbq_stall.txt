[Exploration Log]

I discovered this place while lost.

Deep within the bamboo grove is not somewhere one would linger for long.
Directions become unreliable, and paths are difficult to follow.
Yet in such a place, there stands a small barbecue stall.

The structure is simple.
Wooden racks, charcoal tools, and neatly arranged ingredients.
Everything appears to be in regular use.

The problem is—there is no one here.

No footsteps, no signs of departure.
The charcoal has already gone out, but the scent of recent use still lingers.
The ingredients on the table are fresh, as if just prepared.

I did not stay long.

——

[Supplementary Record]

In the following days, I attempted to return to this area at night.

From a distance, a faint glow could be seen.
As if someone had lit the charcoal.

But when I approached—

there was still no one there.

The stall remained in the same state,
as though it continues to “operate” every night.

Its method of operation remains unknown.
