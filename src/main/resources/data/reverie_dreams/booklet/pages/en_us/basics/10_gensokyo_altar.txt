<yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:gensokyo_altar'></> is an essential crafting workstation in this mod, and most items are created using it.
The altar supports crafting recipes composed of nine items.
To function properly, the following structure must also be built:

### Image: reverie_dreams:gensokyo_altar_example Example structure of <item 'reverie_dreams:gensokyo_altar'>.

The surrounding pillars of the structure must be made from <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:stripped_spiritual_log'></>.
This type of wood can typically be found in <green>Savanna</> biomes.

The top layer logs must be blessed using <item 'reverie_dreams:paper'> with <key 'key.use'>.
To remove the blessing, use shears with <key 'key.use'>.

Finally, after confirming the recipe is correct, use “empty hand + Shift + right-click” to complete the crafting.
