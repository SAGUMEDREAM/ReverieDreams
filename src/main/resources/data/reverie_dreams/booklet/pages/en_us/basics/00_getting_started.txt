Gensokyo: Reverie of Lost Dreams is a magic adventure server-side mod that draws inspiration from various high-quality Touhou fan mods and games, bringing a wide range of Touhou Project content into modern Minecraft.
<nl2>
To begin your journey, you should first craft the <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:gensokyo_altar'></>. It is used to create items such as <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:horai-dama_no_eda'></>, <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:gungnir'></>, and <yellow><item 'reverie_dreams:hakurei_cane'></>.
<nl2>
<red><item 'reverie_dreams:power'></> and <blue><item 'reverie_dreams:point'></> are among the basic crafting materials. They can be obtained by defeating monsters or fairies.
<nl2>
From there, you can continue to obtain and craft more items. You may learn more through this guide, or check their recipes via Polydex.
